# Privacy Policy for Chemistry Lab

Chemistry Lab is a mixed reality educational prototype for Meta Quest devices.

## Data We Use

Chemistry Lab may use Meta User ID to identify participants in a local multiplayer session and to enable shared spatial anchor functionality.

Chemistry Lab may use Meta User Profile, such as username, only to distinguish participants in a shared session.

## Multiplayer Service

Chemistry Lab may use Photon Engine to provide multiplayer networking and synchronize gameplay state, such as object ownership and object transforms.

## How We Use Data

Data is used only to support local multiplayer mixed reality sessions, shared spatial anchor alignment, and gameplay synchronization.

## Data We Do Not Use

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or selling to third parties.

## Data Sharing

Limited multiplayer session data may be processed by Photon Engine for networking functionality. We do not sell personal data.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer of Chemistry Lab.
